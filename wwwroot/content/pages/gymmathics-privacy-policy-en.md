# GymMathics Privacy Policy EN


**Effective date:** September 1, 2026
**Last updated:** September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **GymMathics** app ("App", "we")
handles your information. The App is developed by **Novrian**
("Developer") and is available on the Google Play Store under the
package name `com.novrian.gymmathics`.

By downloading and using the App, you agree to the practices described
in this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the App.

---

## 1. Quick Summary

- GymMathics is a basic math practice app.
- **We do not operate our own server.** All of your practice data (player
  name, level progress, XP, and session history) is stored **only
  locally on your device**.
- We **do not** require you to create an account, and we **do not**
  collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts,
  precise location, photos, or other identity data.
- The App shows ads through **Google AdMob** and supports in-app
  purchases through **Google Play Billing**. These third-party services
  may collect certain data as described below.

---

## 2. Information We Process

### 2.1 Information you provide yourself (stored locally)

| Data | Example | Purpose | Storage location |
|------|---------|---------|-------------------|
| Player name | The nickname you freely type on the Profile page | Greeting you in the app and displaying it on result cards | A file in the App's internal storage on your device |
| Progress & achievements | Levels passed, stars, XP, rank | Saving your game progress | A file in the App's internal storage |
| Session history | Operation, mode/level, score, duration, stars, session date | Displaying your practice history | A file in the App's internal storage |
| "Pro" status | A flag indicating whether ads have been disabled via purchase | Enabling the ad-free version | A file in the App's internal storage |

Data in this section is **never sent to the Developer** and **never
uploaded to any server** by us. The player name is entirely up to you;
we recommend **not** using your full legal name or other sensitive
information.

This data is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear the App's data
through your device settings (see Section 8).

### 2.2 Information collected automatically by third-party services

**a. Google AdMob (advertising)**

The App shows banner and interstitial ads from Google AdMob. To serve
and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and
process data such as:

- Your device's **Advertising ID (Google Ad ID)**;
- IP address and approximate IP-based location (e.g., country/city);
- Device and system information (model, OS version, language, carrier,
  network type);
- Ad interactions (ads viewed, tapped, and view duration);
- Identifiers used for fraud prevention and ad frequency capping.

This data is collected directly by Google through the Google Mobile Ads
SDK embedded in the App. Its processing is governed by
**[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)** and
**[how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its
services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)**.

**b. Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)**

If you purchase the "Pro" product (remove ads), the transaction is
processed entirely by **Google Play**. We **do not** receive or store
your card number, payment data, or identity. The App only asks Google
Play whether the currently signed-in account owns the "Pro" product,
then stores that status flag locally. Ownership is tied to your Google
Play account by Google, so the purchase can be restored on another
device using the same account. Payment processing is governed by the
**[Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/)**.

**c. Google Play Services**

Distribution, updates, integrity verification, and platform-level crash
reporting may be handled by Google Play in accordance with Google's
policies.

We do **not** embed any other third-party analytics, behavioral
tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics,
Crashlytics, Facebook SDK) in the App.

## 3. Basis and Purpose of Processing

We and our third-party service providers process the information above
for the following purposes:

- Running the App's core functionality (storing your progress and
  history on-device);
- Displaying ads to fund the App for free-tier users;
- Processing and restoring "Pro" purchases;
- Maintaining security and preventing abuse and ad fraud;
- Complying with applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Sharing and Saving Result Images

The App has "Share Result" and "Download Result" features on the
results page.

- **Download Result** saves a PNG image of the result card to your
  device gallery, in the `Pictures/GymMathics` album. The image is only
  saved on your device.
- **Share Result** creates a temporary image and opens Android's
  built-in share menu. You fully choose the destination (messaging app,
  social media, email, etc.). Once the image leaves GymMathics, its
  handling is governed by the destination app's privacy policy.

We do not upload result images to any server.

## 5. App Permissions

| Permission | Reason for use |
|------------|-----------------|
| `INTERNET` | Loading ads from Google AdMob and communicating with Google Play Billing |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Checking network availability before requesting an ad |
| `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (Android 9 / API 28 and below only) | Saving result images to the gallery on older Android versions; on Android 10 and above, the save-to-gallery feature uses scoped storage and does not require this permission |

The App does **not** request location, camera, microphone, contacts,
phone, SMS, or broad file-access permissions.

## 7. Personalized Ads and Your Choices

- You can limit or reset your Advertising ID via
  **Device Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
  There you can also choose **"Delete advertising ID"** or
  **"Opt out of Ads Personalization"**.
- In regions where required (e.g., the EU/EEA, UK), the App may show a
  Google consent dialog before personalized ads are served.
- You can remove ads entirely by purchasing the **"Pro"** product within
  the App.

---

## 8. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

- The data described in Section 2.1 is stored on your device for as
  long as the App is installed.
- To delete all of the App's local data, you can:
  1. Go to **Device Settings → Apps → GymMathics → Storage → Clear
     data**, or
  2. Uninstall the App.
- Data collected by Google (Section 2.2) is stored and deleted according
  to Google's retention policies. Requests regarding that data can be
  made through your Google Account's privacy controls at
  https://myaccount.google.com/.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

GymMathics is intended for a general audience wanting to practice basic
arithmetic skills. We do **not** knowingly collect personal data from
children. Because the App does not ask for a real name, email, or other
identity data, and all game data is stored locally, the App does not
build a user profile on our side.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided
personal data to a third-party service through the App, please contact
us at the address in Section 12, and use the Advertising ID controls in
Section 7 to limit ad data collection.

---

## 10. Security

Local data is stored in the App's private storage area, which on
standard Android devices cannot be accessed by other apps. However, no
method of electronic storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee
absolute security, particularly on rooted or modified devices.
