# Privacy Policy — Quark Loop

**Effective date:** October 28, 2026
**App:** Quark Loop : Synth Game Game
**Developer:** Novrian

---
Quark Loop ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android by Novrian ("I", "me"). The game itself works fully offline. It shows ads through Google AdMob when the device is online.

## What the game collects

I do not collect your personal data, and I have no server, account or login. The game has no analytics or tracking of its own.

The only data collection comes from the advertising SDK (Google Mobile Ads SDK, by Google). When an ad is requested or shown, the SDK and Google may collect and use:

- your IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived),
- a device or advertising identifier (the Android advertising ID, and an app set ID),
- ad interactions, such as ad views and taps,
- diagnostic information, such as app launch time and the SDK's performance.

Google uses this to serve and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. I do not receive this data and cannot identify you from it. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Ads

- A full-screen ad may appear when you move on to the next level.
- Optionally, you can watch a rewarded ad to get a hint. You are never required to watch one.
- Ads are shown only while the device is online. Without a connection the game plays normally, with no ads and no hints.
- Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

## Your choices

- **Consent.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent (through Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads that use your data are requested. Your choice is stored by the SDK on your device.
- **Personalized ads.** You can turn these off, and reset or delete your advertising ID, in Android Settings under Privacy > Ads (the exact path depends on your device).

## What stays on your device

Your progress (solved levels, stars, daily streak, chosen language, sound kit, board theme, and a counter that paces ads) is saved in a small file in the game's private storage on your device. I cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect its own information about installs and crashes under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). That is outside my control.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new date.
